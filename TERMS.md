# Terms & Conditions — USM Mod Notifier

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026

## 1. Overview

USM Mod Notifier is an internal moderation utility developed to help the r/USMobile moderation team stay informed about community activity.

The application provides timely Slack notifications for new Reddit posts, comments, and discussions that reach certain engagement levels.

## 2. Purpose of the Application

Reddit's native notification features may not always provide the visibility needed to monitor ongoing community discussions efficiently.

USM Mod Notifier helps the moderation team:

- Receive timely notifications about new posts and comments.
- Identify Trending, Hot, and Viral discussions based on comment activity.
- Monitor community conversations more efficiently.
- Respond to user questions, concerns, and service-related discussions.

## 3. Intended Use

The application is intended for authorized moderation and community support activities associated with r/USMobile.

It is not designed as a general-purpose Reddit monitoring service or a commercial service offered directly to Reddit users.

The application must be used in accordance with Reddit's applicable policies and developer requirements.

## 4. Notifications and Functionality

USM Mod Notifier monitors supported Reddit events and forwards relevant notifications to designated Slack channels.

Notification delivery depends on the availability and operation of Reddit's Developer Platform, Slack, and other supporting services.

Although the application aims to provide timely notifications, uninterrupted delivery or immediate notification of every event cannot be guaranteed.

## 5. Third-Party Services
